REM ============================================================
REM Script 14: INSTANT BACKSPACE BURST (non-malicious)
REM Purpose: "unsophisticated correction" signature — tests
REM backspace_ratio and dwell/flight timing when deletion happens
REM as one contiguous, zero-delay block rather than being woven
REM into normal typing.
REM Behavioural signature:
REM  - All 36 BACKSPACE presses fire back-to-back at raw HID
REM    speed (REPEAT with no DELAY between repeats) — inter-key
REM    interval near the HID polling floor, same signature class
REM    as Script 1's zero-delay burst.
REM  - The entire deletion happens as a single uninterrupted
REM    block immediately after the line finishes, with no
REM    "hesitation" delay before the first BACKSPACE — a real
REM    human noticing a typo almost always pauses briefly first.
REM  - Deletion count exactly matches the line length (no
REM    overshoot/undershoot), which real correction behaviour
REM    rarely does.
REM ============================================================
DELAY 500
GUI r
DELAY 300
STRINGLN notepad
DELAY 800

STRING This line will be deleted instantly.
DELAY 200
BACKSPACE
REPEAT 35
DELAY 300
STRINGLN This is the clean sentence typed after the instant backspace burst removed the line above.
